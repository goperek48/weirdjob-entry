One weird job title for make.fun's "Weirdest Job Title: Blind Judge Showdown" bounty ($5, top 10 paid). A blind judge compares titles two at a time and picks the weirder, funnier one.

The title: **"Senior Vice President of Apologizing to the Printer"** (51 characters).

A real-sounding corporate title handed one gloriously pointless duty: apologizing to the office printer. Everybody has wanted to do it; nobody has been paid to do it.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```

`main.py` (Python 3.13, stdlib only) writes the title to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (`/output/entry.txt` by default). It re-checks the gate itself first: UTF-8, 1–60 characters, more than blank space, exactly one line.
